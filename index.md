# Hello, welcome to my website!

I am an MPhil Economics candidate at the University of Cape Coast, Ghana, and a professional teacher under the Ghana Education Service. My research lies at the intersection of **international trade and finance, sustainability, higher education student affairs, and macroeconomics**. I am an African Economic Research Consortium (AERC) Collaborative Masters Programme (CMAP) scholar.
